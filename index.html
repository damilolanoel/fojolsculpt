Who We Are

Built on Craft,
Driven by Vision.

FOJOL Sculpt is a duly registered Nigerian sculpture design and production company, headquartered in Lagos. We specialise in the conceptualisation, fabrication, and installation of high-quality sculptural works for public, corporate, religious, and private clients.

Our team combines artistic mastery with structural engineering discipline — ensuring every commission is not just beautiful, but built to endure.

The Oba Akran Statue stands as a landmark cultural monument commissioned by the Lagos State Government, erected in Ikeja, Lagos. This project represents FOJOL Sculpt's capacity to execute culturally significant, large-scale public works with precision, professionalism, and lasting artistic integrity.
